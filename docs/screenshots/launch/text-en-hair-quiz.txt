BEFORE YOU START
What matters when choosing hair care
Scalp oil
Fast-oiling scalps need enough cleansing power; slower or drier scalps do better with gentle surfactants.
Flakes and itch
Dandruff responds to proven actives such as piroctone olamine or zinc pyrithione; fragrance and essential oils can make a sensitive scalp itchier.
Hair condition
Damage, color, perms, fine or fragile hair decide how much repair and conditioning you need.
Environment
Hard water means less lather and more residue, so chelating agents help; humidity and season change how oily or dry your scalp gets.
