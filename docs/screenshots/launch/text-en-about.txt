Disclaimer and privacy
Disclaimer
Recommendations here come from fixed rules applied to public information. They are for reference only, are not medical advice, and do not replace a doctor's diagnosis or treatment. No AI is used to generate recommendations.
The "health notes" on the results page are prompts, not diagnoses. If your scalp stays red, hurts, oozes, you lose noticeably more hair, or things get worse, consider seeing a dermatologist.
Scoring rules and weights are initial settings and will be adjusted based on feedback. A score shows how well a product fits what you told us, not how good the product is.
"Brand claims" come from brand product pages and "data classification" is how we categorized a product; neither is an independently verified effect.
"Fragrance-free" and "no fragrance ingredient found" are based on ingredient lists and do not guarantee the absence of allergens. If you are prone to allergies, patch test first.
Ingredient lists, prices and sizes come from public brand and retailer pages and may have changed; formulas can also differ by region and batch. Check the packaging and the seller's page before buying.
We do not sell products, show ads or take affiliate commissions. Purchase links go to third-party retailers, who are responsible for prices, stock and after-sales service.
Privacy
Your answers and profiles are stored only in the browser you are using (local storage), and recommendations are calculated in your browser. Nothing is uploaded to our servers. Your language setting is also stored locally.
No account is needed, and we do not collect names, email addresses or phone numbers. Profile names (like "Me" or "Mom") stay on your device.
The site's code contains no analytics, advertising or tracking scripts, and uses no cookies to record your activity.
The site is hosted on Cloudflare Pages. Like any website, the hosting provider may keep standard access logs (such as IP address, browser type and time of visit); these logs do not include your answers.
Purchase links and source links take you to other websites, whose privacy policies apply.
To clear your data, delete profiles on the Profile page or clear this site's data in your browser settings. Profiles do not sync across browsers or devices.

Data sources
Ingredient basics: the European Commission's CosIng database (retrieved 2026-10-17); fragrance allergens: Annex III of the EU Cosmetics Regulation.
Products: public brand and retailer pages; each product's details list its sources and review date.
Water hardness: public water quality reports from local utilities, valid only for the system or sampling point they describe.
This project is open source; the scoring rules and all data can be viewed in the code repository.
